## Recording the voice-over in iMovie (Mac, free)

1. Open iMovie, create a new movie, and drag `careops-hub-demo.mp4` into the timeline.
2. Move the playhead to the start of your first line (for example 0:00 for Brahim, 0:28 for Sarah).
3. Click the microphone icon under the viewer (Record voiceover), then the red button. Read your line while the video plays, and click again to stop.
4. Do the same for each of your lines. If a take is wrong, delete that clip and record it again.
5. When both voices are in: File > Share > File, then save the MP4.

Recording separately: the first person records their lines and shares the iMovie project or an exported MP4 with the voice; the second person opens it and adds their own lines at their times. Use the same microphone setup if possible, in a quiet room.
